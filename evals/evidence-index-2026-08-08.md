# Evidence index — the sweep behind gap-analysis.md §22

Dated 2026-08-08. Every claim an 18-agent literature sweep returned, with the URL the
agent reported retrieving. This file exists so §22.7's first falsification path — "open
the primaries marked agent-reported" — is executable by someone who was not in the
session.

**Read the last column before trusting a row.** `primary` / `primary PDF` means this
repo's author opened the source and confirmed the numbers. `secondary summary` means only
a summary of the paper was reachable. `agent-reported` means one retrieval agent's reading
of an abstract, unconfirmed — that is most of this file. The verdict tallies after the
slash are the adversarial pass (three lenses: replication, population-transfer, so-what);
a claim with no tally was not load-bearing enough to be sent for verification.

Two errors were caught inside this run and are recorded in §22.1: Marx et al. does not
confine the timing deficit to sub-second durations, and the Bayes factors the completeness
critic attributed to Jylkkä et al. are not in that paper. Assume more remain.
